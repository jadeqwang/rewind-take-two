# v6 eraser + jacket audit

Source: `deliverables/wip/rewind_v6_preview_540p_sfx.mp4` (film time = file time). Pass 1 was 2 fps contact sheets over the whole film. Pass 2 used zoomed, saturation-boosted crops of the source clips at full res (1920x1080 engraved takes), plus a pixel hue count (pink vs pale-blue) on the eraser regions where they were too small to read by eye.

Eraser canon: of Jade's two crossed pencils, one eraser is PALE BLUE and one is PINK. A shot is only reported as a problem if both erasers are visible and they don't match canon.
Jacket canon: (a) a small pale-blue dot on the front, on her LEFT chest; (b) the back is plain white with one large pale-blue dot and no orange (the orange band stops at the side seam under the arm); (c) a "1420 / MHz" roundel on the left sleeve.
