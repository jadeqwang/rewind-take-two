## The one-sentence film

A grad student who can rewind time is hunted by the Time Variance █████████ for the
color she leaves behind, until she realizes the world chasing her is a *drawing*,
and she's the one holding the pencil.

## The idea that holds everything together

1. **The world is a drawing.** Chicago is rendered in black and white, like an Escher woodcut or
   lithograph. Nobody remarks on it, because it's just the art style. The Bean reflects the
   city like Escher's *Hand with Reflecting Sphere* (1935), and Chicago's downtown is literally
   called **the Loop**. The trap ending is hidden in plain sight from frame one.
2. **Jade is the only thing in color.** She's anime, full color, in the canon outfit:
   - white cropped bomber with an orange chest/arm band, a pale-blue dot patch and a 1420 MHz patch
   - black crop top, navy cargo pants with orange straps, chunky white sneakers
   - orange headphones around her neck
   - long black hair, worn **up in a bun with a pencil stuck through it** (planted, never mentioned)
3. **Color = causal residue.** Every rewind leaves a ribbon of her color behind (orange to sky
   blue). The residue is how the TVA tracks her: the more she rewinds, the more color in the
   world, and the easier she is to find. That's why "Anomaly detected" lands.
4. **Each rewind spawns a ghost.** Following Braid World 5's shadow mechanic and Super Meat
   Boy's all-attempts replay, every past iteration keeps running as a translucent colored
   Jade. These ghosts **are** the committee of variant Jades: the iterations of her that
   didn't make it, still running her old routes, helping where they can.
5. **The turn: she stops running from the drawing and starts drawing.** At "Anomaly detected"
   time freezes in a weightless music-box glitch. She pulls the pencil from her hair, her hair
   falls loose, and she redraws a connection (Escher's *Drawing Hands*, strange loop). The map
   rotates like Monument Valley, paths reconnect, and the agents end up marching around
   *Ascending and Descending*, Penrose stairs bolted onto the Loop's elevated tracks, forever.
   Everywhere she redraws turns to color, still in pencil.
6. **"it couldn't trace."** By the final drop the whole world is in color. Residue only
   identifies her when it's rare, so once everything is color the TVA has nothing left to trace her by.
   She wins with perspective, not speed.

## Genre ladder (it changes at every musical genre change)

| Music | Game genre | Visual grammar |
|---|---|---|
| Intro + Verse 1 (drumless, rubato) | **Cold open / cutscene.** A flash of the death, rewound. | Letterboxed woodcut night drive, slow and dreamlike. Lyrics live in the world (LSD signs, mirror, ID card). |
| Pre-drop "shot … and time stops" | **Game over** | Body shot, never the head (she needs her brain to rewind). Freeze. She shatters into colored motes, like a Celeste death. |
| Tape stop / silence | **Pause menu** | `▶ RESUME / ◀◀ REWIND / QUIT`. The cursor moves to REWIND. |
| Drop 1 (half-time, underwater) | **Braid rewind** | The whole film so far runs backward at half speed under a tinted ripple, with a ◀◀ ×2 ×4 ×8 HUD and her residue ribbon unspooling. |
| Verse 2 (dark minimal trap) | **Precision platformer** (Celeste / Super Meat Boy) | Side-scroll on LSD → Grant Park, with dash, wall-jump and dodge roll. Death counter, speedrun timer, a ghost racer (the shadow "like it's a race"). "Reload" = a LOADING screen with a tip. |
| Build | **Bullet-hell gauntlet** | Kaizo-trap density, cuts every beat, then death #2. |
| Drop 2 (trance + string ostinato) | **Braid shadow / co-op** | Deliberate rewinds. Each one spawns a ghost Jade, the committee grows, and color residue floods the frame. Euphoric. |
| "Anomaly detected" | **System alert** | TVA HUD goes red. Every agent turns to camera at once. |
| Glitch break (music box, clock backwards) | **Puzzle reveal** | Weightless. The Bean reflection shows the impossible geometry it always held. The pencil comes out. |
| Verse 3 (drumless, intimate) | **Monument Valley** | Isometric Chicago diorama, orthographic camera, rotating to reconnect paths. Agents follow and loop. Color blooms where she draws. |
| Final drop (orchestral DnB, key change up) | **Boss fight, every genre at once** | Full speed plus every ghost Jade running together. The world flips (Escher *Relativity*) on the key change. Boss = the TVA clock (Dalí's melted clock as its tell). Kandinsky / Klimt color crescendo. |
| Outro (trailer impacts) | **Trailer cards** | One frozen tableau per impact, then a hard cut to black. |
| Whisper "Never stop." | **New Game+** | The iteration counter ticks over. Pencil back in her hair. |

## Rules (lessons from take one)

- **One idea per frame.** Jade large in frame. At most one HUD layer, plus the lyric.
  Take one died of 3–5 stacked type/HUD layers per frame and Jade as a small inset.
- The agency's name is always **TIME VARIANCE █████████**. Never write "Authority", "TVA" or Marvel terms on screen.
- No head shots, ever. The "shot" is a body hit, and we cut on the whisper.
- Singing Jade appears at most 3 times, all at the Bean, in colored-pencil style, on the most
  important phrases only.
- Easter eggs are never pointed at: a hotel sign on Lake Shore reads **HILBERT** (no vacancy
  / always vacancy), a melted clock, *Drawing Hands*, *Relativity*, *Ascending and Descending*,
  and a Banach–Tarski split (one Jade → two Jades) during the boss fight.
