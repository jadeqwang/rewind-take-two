# Section C: Verse 2, "the precision platformer" (V2a–V2n, 56.54–112.96 s)

Director: section C agent. Budget $70. Status: see "Status" at the bottom (kept current).

## Direction

Reference energy: Guy Collins' *Kaizo Trap*. Every action cut is one legible near-miss or trap, 1–2 beats long, and
almost every trap comes from somewhere unexpected (the road tilts, a sedan flies over, a lamppost falls, trucks close,
an L train drops from the sky). The three wordless "oh" swells, the bridge hang and "Reload." get room to breathe.

- **Grammar.** The car chase and the on-foot chase use **side-scroll**: camera perpendicular to the motion, tracking
  sideways at her speed, the car or Jade moving left to right (forward = screen right, always). The grass field (V2j)
  is **top-down**. Hero shots on the swells may break the grammar (low angles, high wide) because they are the
  section's "cutscene" beats.
- **Night → day.** The car chase stays at night (continuity with Verse 1: engraved black sky, lights and siren strobes as
  white engraved starbursts). "Reload." is a level change: the respawn level (L tracks, Grant Park) is the bright
  paper-white engraving of the style tests. The LOADING screen makes the jump legal.
- **Car.** A white 2001 Acura TL sedan, drawn as mostly bare paper with fine contour lines so it reads as the white car
  against the black sedans. Agents' sedans are black with **white engraved starburst strobes on the roof, no color**.
- **The Kaizo L-train smash** (92.79) gets no setup at all: 5 frames of train filling the frame, then 2 frames black.
- **Ghost runner** (V2i) and **frame-skipping agents** (V2k) are engine effects over generated plates: the ghost is
  Jade's own clip, tinted graphite and offset; the stutter holds the background on each beat while Jade (matte) moves
  continuously.

### Deviations from the shot list (and why)

- **V2j ends 104.22 instead of 103.74, V2k starts 104.22 instead of 104.17.** The shot list leaves a 0.43 s hole between
  them; 104.22 is the bar-60 downbeat (one frame after "they" at 104.17).
- **V2b** keeps the four listed traps but each is split into a setup and a payoff cut (2 beats each), with one interior
  insert, so every cut is a single idea.
- **V2c** adds 2-beat action cuts between the three swells so the swells read as held "cutscene" breaths against
  the relentless cutting.
- **V2h/V2i** respawn into the day (paper-white) engraving: the level changed.
- **V2m** uses a side angle on the body (P4 used a front torso cut).

## Status (2026-10-11): done, in the engine

Clips with Jade in them keep their matte. The section is wired into the engine as `render/timeline/C.js` (shots +
HUD + typography). Contact sheet: `docs/sections/C_contact.jpg`.

- Preview (section alone, with audio): `node render/engine/render.mjs --timeline render/timeline/_C_solo.js --range 56.54 112.96 --preview --encode`
  writes `render/engine/out/C_solo/C_solo_preview_56.54-112.96.mp4`. Rendering takes about 25 s.

  `chosen.json` (take, in/out, rate, and **every** use in the cut) and `NOTES.md` (keyframe prompt + model, video

  review helpers `grid.py` and `strip.py`.

### What's weak

- **V2c7 (swell 3)**: the sedans face the camera, so the raised bridge sits *behind* the pack instead of ahead of
  the white car. It still reads as "they fan out behind her", but the geography flips for 1.8 s.
- **V2c2 (swell 1)**: the leaf rises only a little in the clip, and the car is small. It reads as a held,
  dizzying bridge shot rather than a dramatic lift.
- **V2a3** (Jade at the wheel) barely acts. Seedance ignored "yank the wheel / glance at the mirror". It is used for
  two inserts.
- **V2f2** copies section A's D3 layout. Seedance drew red/blue police strobes in the mirror, which the engrave
  pass removes. As in D3, the 1420 MHz patch sits on her near (right) sleeve. If D3 changes, regenerate V2f2 from the
  new D3 keyframe (the `V2f2_n2` job in `kf.py`).
- **V2n1**: she stands *on* a giant arcade panel (surreal scale) instead of an extreme close-up of her hand. It is
  0.3 s and reads as "slam the button".
- **V2i2**: the "slide under the signal arm" became "lie flat while the arm swings over". The cut stops before she
  crawls.
- **Night plates** show line moiré in 960×540 previews. That is downscale aliasing; the 1080p stills are clean.
- Car identity holds well: Seedance kept the white sedan in every take because the keyframes carry it.
- The master `render/timeline.js` currently fails `--list` on a duplicate shot id "O1" between `media/shots/F_edl.json`
  and `render/timeline/E.js`. That is outside section C; section C renders through `_C_solo.js`.
