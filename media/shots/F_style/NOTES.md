4 keyframes (T1 sprint + colour wave, T2 boss clock, T3 Klimt gold, T4 Bean dive) × 2 models (google/nano-banana-pro,
openai/gpt-image-2.5-flare), same prompt (`tools/sectionF/style_test.py`), refs = hair-down sheet, canon turnaround,

- `sheet_style_r1.jpg`: **nano-banana-pro chosen.** The engraving stays on top and the colour sits in the hatching:

  "before"). With full bloom at strength 1 the pencil tint is pale and the paper grays; at strength 0.3–0.5 it adds
  blotches. So colour shots use strength 0 + bloom (upscale + matte only).
