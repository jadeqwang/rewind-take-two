## Decisions that differ from the shot list (and why)

- **C1 composition:** a low angle from behind five agents instead of "agents below". The bullet's wake runs in a straight line from the centre
  agent's muzzle to her ribs, so cause and threat read in one look. Picked from 11 variants (`C1/NOTES_variants.md`).
  It's a short Seedance take (only hair and motes drift) instead of an engine STILL, so living things move.
- **D1 second panel** reads `SOUTH` (it was blank in the plate; a blank sign looked unfinished).
- **D3 etch** sits along the top of the mirror, not the bottom. The bottom strip is bright road, and the frosted etch
  disappeared against it.
- **S2** uses S1's own last frame for the push instead of the clean plate. A crossfade to `plate_close_1_crop` ghosted
  because the take drifts from the first-frame geometry.

## What's weak

- **D5** lens lyric is legible only at full resolution, and the x2.2 push makes the 720p engraving coarse. Options: a
  new keyframe with much bigger sunglasses, or let the lyric fall to the lower third.
- **C4** sticky note is small (about 110 px), so `DEFENSE 9AM` reads, but only just. She blinks at the end of the shot.
- **D8** meter needle moves only slightly; the "slam to max" is mostly carried by the head tilt and the motes.
- **D1** sign text is small for its first 0.5 s before the gantry rushes overhead.
- **P4** whisper *shot* is tiny by design (whisper style); check it on a phone.
- Seedance invented extra characters late in several takes (D1, D5, P1, S2, C3: colourful anime girls after about 1.5–2.5 s).
  All of them fall outside the used ranges. If someone retimes these shots, check the raw takes.
- R2: `03picasso` and `12ice` are a little static, and `13sedanb`'s grille looks like a Mercedes.
