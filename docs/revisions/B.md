There’s some really good ideas in the chase scenes, but we can make them massively better. Take a closer look at Kaizo Trap, online footage of games like Celeste, and super Meat Boy. And then let’s do a pass on thinking deeply about improvements to chase scenes:

* running towards agents doesn’t make sense, the point is to run away from them, and she’s fundamentally a pacifist heroine
* always be running away from them, jump from a train platform onto a steel beam, jump over a chasm, trajectory looks like she’s not about to make it, but then she uses a dash
* “zigzag across the grass” - running inside a circle of agents is tense but unexciting, we should take an actual grassy field in Chicago (perhaps near the Drake Hotel, renamed Hilbert) and she’s zigzagging, and they’ve opened fire but miss her several times, one speaks into an earpiece, and at “just too fast” one of them spawns around the corner from the Hilbert Hotel
* 2:06 - I like the rainbow running Jades, but the placement of where the agents are doesn’t make sense for the directly she’s going. It makes sense for them to be pursuing her and she’s running away from them. It also make sense for this to be a post ‘banach-tarski’ moment.
* 2:07 - the boost: she should actually climb up successfully after the boost (or cut to another scene before we see her drop downwards from the boost)
* 2:08 - ‘banach tarski’ hint - what if we find a more clever way to deliver the hint? just a crazy idea, what if there are giant books on the table and ethereal Jade grabs protagonist Jade’s hand and as they slide, ethereal Jade points out a ‘banach-tarski’ on the spine of a book? then the moment being surrounded by agents would be a good moment to do a dodge roll as agents rush each other, and she atomizes in mid-air and lands as two copies who run in separate directions
