## Styles

Starts drumless and rubato: intimate solo felt piano, harp and fingerpicked acoustic guitar with a breathy close-mic female vocal, percussion only after the first time-stop. Slow-burn crescendo where every time-stop snaps into a new reality and adds a layer: filtered half-time sub-bass drop, dark minimal trap with 808s and sirens, euphoric trance supersaws over a relentless 16th-note spiccato string ostinato, finally epic orchestral drum and bass with taiko, brass and massive choir. Video-game chase energy that keeps escalating to the end. Tape stops, reversed vocals, rewind FX, ticking clocks, glitch stutters. Dark, dreamlike, sci-fi. Minor key, 136 BPM.
