## What v2 gets wrong (from its own frames)

- **She runs at the agents four times:** the E2b field chops (slide, cartwheel, vault, spin), the E2c ring, the E4
  gap jump onto the agents' platform, and the E7 reuses of those shots.
- **The field (V2j) is a ring that closes on her.** It is tense, but she has nowhere to go, so nothing changes from
  beat to beat.
- **There is no geography.** The L tracks, the field, the ring and the roof are separate rooms. We never see what
  she is running toward, so we cannot tell whether she is winning.
- **The boost (E5a) cuts while she is still dropping** from the top of the vault.
- **The hint (E5c) is a held insert of a slip of paper,** which stops the chase for 1.7 s.

## Rules for the restage

1. **Left to right, always.** Agents enter at the left edge, behind her. The goal, the Hilbert Hotel, is visible
   screen-right from the first field shot. The only agent who ever faces her is the one who shoots her.
2. **One level.** Platform, steel beam, chasm, lawn, hotel corner, hotel wall. Verse 2 plays it once at full length.
   The 13 chops replay it as fast retries. The rainbow run plays it a third time.
3. **The shadow fails first.** The graphite shadow of the last attempt jumps the chasm and falls short. She takes the
   same jump in the same framing and dashes.
4. **The dash is Celeste's.** Arc visibly dropping with the far lip above her feet, a 2-frame freeze, a white ring, a
   flat dash with three afterimages, a skid landing. It is spent once at full length, at the chasm.
5. **They shoot where she was.** Each shot lands a quarter of a second behind her, on the beat, and leaves a scar.
   There are no visible bullets (rev-A's canon): a muzzle starburst, then turf.
6. **A miss is announced; the hit is not.** Before each miss a thin ruled trace line runs from the pistol to the
   spot she has just left. The agent at the corner has no line. That is "with no warning".
7. **Copies behave like the replay.** Stacked as one, smeared into a rainbow bar, fanned at the jump.

Two things from the references I am not taking:

- **Scale.** Kaizo keeps her at a seventh of frame height and Celeste at 6–8%. The treatment's rule from take one is
  "Jade large in frame", and a figure that small does not survive 720p generation. Side-scroll wides will hold her
  at about a third of frame height.
- **A dash light on her outfit** (Celeste's hair turning blue). Her jacket is canon and does not change colour. The
  afterimages carry the pale blue instead.

### Why this order

Jade's 2:06 note wants the rainbow Jades after the split, and the hint has to come before the split. The two
"Rewind." lines and the bar positions are fixed, so the rainbow run moves from "Rewind." #2 (123.00) to the
instrumental bars at 134.00, where it becomes the climb into "Anomaly detected". "Rewind." #2 takes the boost
instead: a rewound self helping is what that word means in this film. The hint and the split keep their v2 slots.
